About Us

At New Panacea Hospital, we are dedicated to delivering excellence in healthcare through advanced diagnostics, expert surgical care, and compassionate service. With a team of highly experienced medical professionals and state-of-the-art technology, we aim to provide accurate diagnoses and effective treatments tailored to each patient’s unique needs.

Our diagnostic center is equipped with the latest imaging and testing technologies, including 4D Ultrasonography, 24/7 Dialysis Care, CT Scans, Digital X-Rays, ECG, EEG, and comprehensive pathology and microbiology services. These facilities ensure timely, precise, and reliable results to support swift and informed medical decisions.

We also specialize in a wide range of surgical and treatment services — from general and laparoscopic surgeries to joint replacements and complex reconstructive procedures. Our minimally invasive techniques promote faster recovery, reduced discomfort, and better outcomes for our patients.
